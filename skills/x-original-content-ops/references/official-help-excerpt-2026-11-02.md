## Original Content Rewards

Earn payouts for creating original, high-quality content on >x< through the Original Content Rewards Program.

Enroll in Creator Studio: https://x.com/i/jf/creators/studio

### What is the Original Content Rewards Program?

The Original Content Rewards Program is designed to reward creators who produce original, high-quality content. Content in any format – from a post or Article to a video or image – that reflects your own voice, expertise, or creativity. The program is designed to reward creators who bring original ideas, perspectives, expertise, and creativity to >x<.

### What does this mean for Creator Revenue Sharing?

Starting August 7, 2026, we're no longer accepting new enrollments into Revenue Sharing.

If you’re currently enrolled in Revenue Sharing, you’ll continue earning through September 7, 2026. You’ll receive three final payouts: two on August 14 and August 28, in line with the standard payout schedule, and a final payout for earnings accrued through September 7, expected on or around September 11.

Starting September 8, 2026, we'll begin rolling out access for existing Revenue Sharing members to apply for the new Original Content Rewards Program. To join, you'll need to meet the program's eligibility requirements. You can check your eligibility status at any time by going to Creator Studio → Original Content Rewards.

If you've already completed ID verification and have a valid payout method connected to your account, you won't need to complete those steps again. Once approved, you'll continue earning under the Original Content Rewards Program and its Terms and Conditions.

If your account's monetization is currently paused due to a previous policy violation, you will not be eligible to enroll in the Original Content Rewards Program at this time.

### How does the program work?

Eligible creators earn payouts based on qualified impressions generated by their original content. Payouts are issued every two weeks as long as your account and content continue to meet the program's requirements.

### What are qualified impressions?

Qualified impressions are unique impressions from Premium users (subscribers to >x< Premium Basic, Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business) on the Home Timeline feed, where at least 50% of the post is visible.

The following are excluded from qualified impressions:

- Impressions from the same account counted more than once per post
- Paid, promoted, or artificially generated impressions
- Fraudulent impressions

### What is considered original content under this program?

Original content is content you have personally created – written, filmed, designed, or produced – that reflects your own voice, perspective, or expertise.

Commentary is at the heart of >x<. Reacting to, analyzing, or building on what's happening in the world is a legitimate and valued form of original expression. Content that adds your genuine perspective, whether that's breaking down a news story, sharing your take on a cultural moment, or adding context others don't have, counts as original under this program.

What doesn't count is content where your contribution is minimal or absent. If the primary value comes from someone else's work and you've done little to transform or add to it, it won't generate qualified impressions under the program.

Specifically, the following content types are not considered original content under the Original Content Rewards Program:

- Copied content: Text, images, or videos copied entirely from other creators or sources without any original contribution. This includes content that is downloaded from >x< or other platforms and then reuploaded onto >x<.
- Minimally modified content: Content reproduced from others with only slight changes, such as changing a word or two in a post, adding a filter, adjusting video speed, or placing a text overlay, that don't meaningfully transform the original.
- Aggregated content: Posts, videos, or images that primarily compile or combine content from other creators or sources without adding substantial new perspective or framing.
- Cross-platform reposts: Content taken from other platforms and reposted by someone other than the original creator.

### Original Content and Intellectual Property

These Original Content Rewards guidelines are separate from >x<'s intellectual property policies.

Content may qualify as original under this program and still violate our intellectual property rules. Creators are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights, permissions, or licenses to use content created by others.

As a reminder, posting content that infringes another person's intellectual property rights is not permitted on >x<, regardless of whether the content would otherwise qualify as original under this program.

### Eligibility Requirements

To join the Original Content Rewards Program, you must meet all of the following criteria at the time of application:

- Location: Be based in a country where the Original Content Rewards Program is available. See below for a list of available countries.
- Age: Be 18+ years of age.
- Account standing: Have an >x< account in good standing with no history of repeatedly violating our Monetization Guidelines or Terms of Service.
- Account type: Have a Personal or Business Account. Accounts belonging to political or government organizations are not eligible.
- Premium subscription: Hold an active >x< Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business subscription, or a Plus, Premium, Super, or Ultra plan on The X Subscription.
- Impressions: Have at least 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days (impressions on replies are excluded).
- Followers: Have at least 500 verified followers.
- Original content: Actively post original content as defined in this policy.

Meeting these requirements does not guarantee admission to the Program. Once you meet all eligibility requirements, you can submit a quick application to be considered for the Program. We’ll review it and notify you of the outcome.

If your application is not approved, you may submit one appeal. If the appeal is unsuccessful, you can reapply after 42 days, provided you continue to meet all eligibility requirements.

### Continuous Eligibility

After joining the program, you must continue to meet the following requirements to remain eligible and receive payouts, including but not limited to:

- Be subscribed to >x< Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business, or to a Plus, Premium, Super, or Ultra plan on The X Subscription.
- Post original and authentic content.
- Avoid malicious or fraudulent activities.
- Do not tamper with the program, payout system, or recommendation algorithms.
- Do not use automated tools, bots, or any tool or software to artificially generate likes, follows, views, comments, or shares.
- Do not make false statements, produce abusive content, or engage in illegal activities in connection with the program.
- Do not solicit engagements: repeatedly instructing users to engage with posts, such as asking to like, reply, bookmark, follow, or repost.

If your account is found to be in violation of any of the above, you will receive a notification. Depending on the severity of the violation, your account may be temporarily or permanently removed from the Original Content Rewards Program. You may appeal this decision.

The requirements above are not exhaustive. >x< reserves the right to determine eligibility and take enforcement action for conduct that undermines the integrity, safety, or intended purpose of the program, even if such conduct is not specifically listed above.

### Account and Content Requirements

To receive payouts under the Original Content Rewards Program, your content must also meet the following standards, including but not limited to:

- Be original content as defined above
- Receive impressions from Premium accounts on the Home Timeline
- Comply with our Monetization Standards, Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, and Copyright Policy

Content will be ineligible if:

- It is copied or substantially reproduced from another creator
- It is downloaded from >x< or another platform and reuploaded onto >x< (unless you are the author of that content)
- It is inappropriate, sexually explicit, or potentially harmful to viewers
- It is exclusively focused on monetization coaching, monetization discussion, or maximizing payouts
- Was created or posted using automated means
- Contains disinformation or misleading content
- Has a helpful Community Note
- Violates our Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, or Copyright Policy

We periodically review accounts in the Original Content Rewards Program for ongoing compliance. If your account is removed and you believe it's a mistake, you can submit an appeal here.

### Payouts

Payouts are currently processed every two weeks and the minimum payout is $30. Once you are eligible, you’ll need to complete the following steps in Creator Studio to receive payouts:

- For U.S. users: Payouts are made through >x< Money. Ensure you have an active >x< Money account to access your payouts. Get started here.
- For non-U.S. users: Connect a Stripe payout account and complete identity verification via Stripe

Common questions:

Can I connect more than one >x< account to >x< Money?
No. Each >x< Money account can be connected to only one >x< account. You can’t link multiple >x< accounts to the same >x< Money account.

Can I have more than one >x< Money account?
No. Each person can have only one >x< Money account. This is based on your Social Security Number, so opening another >x< account does not let you open another >x< Money account.

### Suspension from Program

If >x< has evidence to believe you may have violated our rules, your account will be suspended from the Original Content Rewards Program. Please review our Creator Monetization Standards prior to appealing. If you're confident you haven't breached these terms, submit an appeal here.

Additionally, effective March 3, 2026 users who post AI-generated videos of an armed conflict without adding a disclosure that it was made with AI will be suspended from the Original Content Rewards Program for 90 days. Subsequent violations will result in a permanent suspension of further payments. We may adjust our policy as the armed conflict situation evolves.

### Withdrawal

To withdraw from Original Content Rewards, please message @Subscriptions for support.
